# SILAH – Legal Services Mobile Application

## Project Idea

**SILAH** is a legal services mobile application that helps users find suitable licensed lawyers for their legal cases. Users can describe their legal issue, and the **AI** analyzes the case to identify the relevant legal specialization and recommend suitable lawyers. Users can then view lawyer profiles, submit a consultation request, and communicate with the selected lawyer through real-time chat.

The application also provides **training opportunities for law graduates**, while lawyers can manage consultation requests, provide training opportunities, and communicate with clients and trainees. Administrators manage lawyer verification, legal specializations, and support requests.

## Main Features

- AI-powered legal issue analysis and lawyer recommendations.
- Lawyer search and professional profiles.
- Legal consultation requests.
- Real-time communication between users and lawyers.
- Lawyer verification and administration.
- Training opportunities for law graduates.
- Training application management.
- Notifications and account management.
- Light and Dark themes.
